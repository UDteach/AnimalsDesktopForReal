Which Mac do I have?

Open the Apple menu and choose “About This Mac.” Check “Chip” or “Processor.” Choose the Intel download if you see Intel, or the Apple Silicon download if you see M1 or a later M-series chip.

After downloading

On Mac, open the DMG and move the app to Applications. On Windows, extract the ZIP and launch the app. The builds are unsigned, so your operating system may show a warning on first launch.
