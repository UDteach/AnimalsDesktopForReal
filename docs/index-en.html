HOW IT WORKS

Small visits, now and then.

We studied how each animal looks and moves, then made short appearances to suit each species.

01

Distinctive motion

Chinchillas hop, hamsters explore, guinea pigs trot, and rabbits make short hops. Sugar gliders glide across your screen.

02

Your desktop stays yours

Animals appear in a transparent overlay. Your wallpaper remains, and mouse clicks pass through.

03

Choose your visitors

Pick one variant, a selected group, or all of them. Set the timing and size for everyone or for each animal. Show them on one monitor or all monitors.

Pomodoro brings them back for breaks.

Automatic visits pause while you focus and resume when a break starts. Defaults are 25 minutes of focus, 5 minutes of break, and a 15-minute break every fourth round. You can change the times and show a small countdown.
